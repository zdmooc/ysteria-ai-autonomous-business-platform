2. MVP gate: authorize expenses per evidence of real product value and compliant go-to-market.
3. Launch gate: activate paid provider plans, channels and campaigns only upon owner approval.

## Metrics
Gross revenue, net revenue ex VAT, transaction fees, platform royalties, refunds, customer acquisition cost, conversion rate, paid churn, LLM/video cost per unit, contribution margin and cash runway.

## Exclusions
No guarantee of earnings. No assumption Amazon KDP has an unrestricted publishing API. No unattended payment initiation, ad spending or marketplace listing without owner-set limits and required consent.
